What your check shares

The public feed shows the title, checklist, reward, wallet addresses, status, receipts, and a rounded map pin. Keep private directions out of the public title and checklist.

Reports are for the participants.

The buyer and assigned scout can read the precise report location, photo, and observations. Photos require authenticated participant access. Their original bytes are preserved so they can be checked against the signed hash.

You choose camera and location access.

ScoutVault asks for camera and foreground location permission when you choose a field capture or nearby search. It does not track your location in the background. Photograph only where you have permission, and leave out faces and private belongings.

Listing and map requests

The listing reader receives the public URL you paste. Do not provide login links or private access tokens. OpenStreetMap receives normal map tile requests for the area you view. Exact report coordinates are not published on chain.

Audio stays optional.

Browser or device speech reads the selected text. Voice availability and network use depend on the installed speech engine. No ElevenLabs connection is used.

Review what the report says.

A wallet signature binds a report to its signing wallet. It does not certify a GPS sensor, photo authenticity, identity, or rental safety. This deployment uses test tokens with no cash value.
